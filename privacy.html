Privacy Policy · Updated 21 November 2026

Your information and how Career-Ops uses it

This notice describes the website's main data flows. Review it before signing in, saving a resume or using optional AI analysis.

1. Information you choose to provide

Visitors may enter role preferences, job tracking notes, resume text, and job descriptions. Some workspace information is kept in the browser's local or session storage. The site also offers an authenticated private resume-text vault and application tracking sync through Supabase; storing records there is optional and tied to your signed-in account. Do not enter secrets or highly sensitive personal data.

2. Sign-in and session data

When you choose Google sign-in, authentication is handled through Google's authorization flow and Supabase. The site receives session credentials needed to access your own authorized records. Session information may be stored in browser session storage. We do not ask for your Google password.

3. Resume files and AI assistance

Where supported, uploaded PDF or DOCX files are parsed in the browser to extract text. When you explicitly use private storage, resume text can be saved to your authenticated account. When you explicitly request an AI review, the resume text, relevant job content and your instructions are sent to a server-side service and an AI provider to process the request. Do not submit confidential third-party documents without permission. Third-party providers may process technical request data under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Public job feed and external sites

Career-Ops displays publicly posted job information from selected employer boards. Clicking an application link takes you to a third-party website. The employer or applicant-tracking platform independently controls the information you provide there. Career-Ops does not automatically submit those forms.

5. Cookies, advertising and similar technologies

The site loads a Google AdSense publisher script. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to measure advertising and, depending on your choices and applicable law, personalize ads. Google's use of advertising cookies can depend on visits to this and other sites. Ads may not display until Google approves the site. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Ads Settings. Browser settings can also manage cookies. Where legally required, consent controls must be configured for ad personalization before serving ads.

6. Hosting, logs, and security

Public website assets are hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting, authentication, database, AI and advertising providers may process network and diagnostic information necessary to provide their services. Access rules are designed to limit authenticated saved records to their owners; no internet-connected system can promise perfect security.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Browser workspace data can be cleared using the site's Settings controls or your browser's storage settings. Clearing browser data does not automatically delete account-stored records. For private stored records, use available account controls or contact us to request review or deletion. Retention may also follow service-provider and legal requirements; we do not claim that clearing one device removes all server-side copies.

8. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests about data associated with your account, contact mchennarao889@gmail.com. Do not post private information in public GitHub issues. We may update this notice as features, providers or legal requirements change, with the updated date shown here.
