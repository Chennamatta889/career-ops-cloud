About this project

Career discovery with clearer next steps.

Career-Ops is a publicly accessible job discovery and career preparation website. Its purpose is to help job seekers research openings, organize their search and prepare more thoughtfully.

What this website offers

The public job feed draws from a curated set of employers that publish Greenhouse and Ashby job-board data. The catalog is not the entire job market, listings may change, and category labels are approximate. Always open the employer's actual posting to verify current requirements.

Visitors can browse IT and non-IT roles, save or track applications, review their resume, and optionally request AI-assisted document feedback through an authenticated private service. The site does not submit job applications automatically and cannot guarantee that any employer will reply or hire.

Why the career guides exist

A job listing is more useful when a visitor knows how to interpret it. These original guides cover practical steps such as analyzing role requirements, troubleshooting technical scenarios, documenting experience, and spotting recruitment scams.

Independence and transparency

Career-Ops is an independent project, not an employer, a recruiting agency, or a representative of the companies appearing in job feeds. Employer trademarks and job links belong to their respective owners. We aim to point readers to authoritative original postings and clearly explain platform limitations.

Feedback

Corrections, broken links, accessibility problems, or privacy questions can be sent through the contact page. Please do not include confidential resumes, passwords or account tokens in public GitHub issues.
